Good to know

Questions fans ask

What is TalkShopLive?

TalkShopLive (TSL) is a live shopping platform where musicians, authors and creators host live videos and sell their newest releases, including signed vinyl, CDs, books, merch and exclusive editions, right inside the stream.

What is a live drop? Is it a concert?

A live drop is a shoppable live video. Some artists perform, some tell the story behind the release, and all of them take fan questions. Either way, everything on screen can be bought during the drop.

Are the autographs real?

Yes. Autographed items are signed by the artist or author, and each listing shows what is signed, such as the cover, an insert or a bookplate.

What if I miss the live?

Replays stay on the site and remain shoppable while items are in stock. You can also set a reminder for any upcoming drop.

Do you sell more than music and books?

Yes. Alongside signed music and books you'll find artist merch, beauty, home and kitchen, and live shop-alongs from brands and makers.

Never miss a signed drop.

Pick what you love and we'll only send drops that match. Signed editions sell out, so alerts are how collectors get there first.
